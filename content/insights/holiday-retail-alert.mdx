## Why it matters

Peak shopping periods increase foot traffic and cash movement. Criminals often probe perimeter weaknesses and after-hours routines.

## Quick checklist

- Verify CCTV coverage on loading bays and rear doors.
- Brief staff on tailgating and suspicious loitering.
- Align key-holder response with your control room escalation path.
