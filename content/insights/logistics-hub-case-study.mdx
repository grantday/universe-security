## Situation

A logistics client faced repeated after-hours breaches near loading zones. Siloed alarm notifications delayed dispatch coordination.

## Approach

- Unified alarm and CCTV signals into the control room.
- GPS-tracked rapid response units with clear dispatch SOPs.
- Daily incident summaries for operations leadership.

## Outcome

Faster first response, clearer accountability, and a repeatable audit trail for insurers and internal compliance.
