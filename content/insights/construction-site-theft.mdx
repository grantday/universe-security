## Layer your deterrence

1. **Controlled access** — Single entry/exit where possible; log vehicles.
2. **Lighting** — Well-lit perimeters reduce opportunistic theft.
3. **CCTV + signage** — Visible cameras signal active monitoring.
4. **Inventory audits** — Weekly spot checks on high-value items.
5. **Response plan** — Pre-agreed escalation to guarding or patrol units.
